## External-model decision

`fasterwam_sota_feasibility.md` records why the newer Faster-WAM checkpoint was
not substituted after outcomes: its released one-pass path reuses a noisy
future cache but does not complete and expose a generated future video, making
it a weaker match to this paper's counterfactual generated-future question.
FastWAM Optional-IDM was frozen first because it generates a future and then
conditions action denoising on that future's cache. No benchmark-rank claim is
made from the overnight action-only study.
